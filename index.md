## About Me

![Profile Picture]({{ "assets/images/Profile_Picture.jpg" | relative_url }}){: class="profile-image"}

I'm an MEng Mechanical Engineering student at the University of Edinburgh, awarded the First Year Class Medal for top performance in my cohort. I recently completed an exchange year at Cornell University (GPA 3.96), and I'm on track to graduate with First-Class honours in 2028.

I'm drawn to sustainable design, engineering analysis, and product development. I like turning early-stage ideas into things that actually work, using software like ANSYS Mechanical, ANSYS Fluent, Python, and Fusion 360 to analyse, simulate, and refine designs before they're built.

In spring 2025, I led my team to 1st place at the BUiD Sustainability Spring School in Dubai, developing a lighting solution with engineers and architects from around the world.
