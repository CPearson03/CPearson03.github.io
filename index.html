About Me

Engineering with purpose

I'm Charlie Pearson, a mechanical engineering student at the University of Edinburgh, currently on exchange at Cornell University. I have a strong interest in mechanical design, dynamics, and analysis — with experience spanning both academic projects and hands-on design work.

My work ranges from designing torque wrenches and suspension systems to studying heat transfer and ecological engineering challenges. I enjoy tackling problems that sit at the intersection of theory and practical application.

I'm always looking for opportunities to apply my skills in new contexts and grow as an engineer. Feel free to explore my projects or get in touch.
